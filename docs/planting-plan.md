# Planting plan — 30-pod layout, nutrients, commissioning

Status: final, owner-decided 2026-07-29 (Trello #219). All-edible — the
flower trial (chamomile, lavender) was cut and the pods reallocated.
Revised later the same day: **lettuce ×4 added (seed on hand)**, trimming
dill and chili by one pod each. Strawberry keeps its 10 pods.

**Tomato is out of the plan entirely (owner, 2026-07-29).** The tros (vine)
tomato added earlier that day was dropped, and with the cherry tomato already
replaced there is now **no tomato in the tower**. Its 2 pods went back to
basil and parsley. Reasons it went: indeterminate at 1.5–2 m with heavy
trusses, it would have shaded the tiers above and dominated reservoir draw in
a 25×25×86 cm tower on 10 L; it also demanded EC 2.0–3.0 against lettuce's
0.8–1.2 on a single shared reservoir. If tomato is ever wanted back, the
tower-appropriate choice is a **dwarf/determinate cherry** (Tiny Tim / Red
Robin) ×2 — seed would need buying — or better, a truss variety in its own
separate bucket with its own solution.

cut-and-come-again. Chili and dill stay at 1 each on purpose: one plant of

Strawberry placement (tier 3 ×5, tier 4 ×3, tier 6 ×2) is unchanged and
matches [seeding/strawberry.md](seeding/strawberry.md).

### Placement rationale

- **Heavy fruiters low (tiers 1–2):** paprika, Dulce Italiano and chili sit
  at the bottom for stability and can be staked from the floor. They will
  crowd sideways as they bulk up — prune/train outward.
- **Strawberries mid + top:** fruit cascades clear of the tower at picking
  height.
- **Cut-and-come-again herbs (tiers 4–5):** basil, parsley.
- **Tall/airy on top (tier 6):** dill needs the headroom.
- **No tomato.** With the tros variety dropped, nothing in the tower now
  exceeds ~40 cm except the dill, so no tier gets shaded out and no plant
  needs external staking or weekly side-shooting.
- **Lettuce is the turnover crop:** ~30–45 days pod-to-plate. Re-sow a
  couple of sponges every 2–3 weeks and the 4 lettuce pods rotate
  continuously.

## Nutrients — one shared reservoir

Single 10 L reservoir → one compromise mix for all crops:

- **EC: 1.4–1.6 mS/cm** — lowered from 1.6–1.8 now that the tomato is gone.
  The truss tomato was the only crop wanting 2.0–3.0 and it was dragging the
  compromise up against lettuce (0.8–1.2) and strawberry (1.2–1.6). Without
  it the heaviest feeders are the peppers (~1.8–2.4), so a **1.4–1.6** middle
  sits much closer to every crop's real optimum.
- **pH: 5.8–6.2, set point ~6.0**

While the tower is **strawberry-only** (first weeks), run the lower
strawberry-optimal **EC ~1.2**, then ramp to 1.4–1.6 as the peppers go in.
Alpines sulk at high EC.

Per-crop ideal hydro pH, all covered by the 5.8–6.2 window:

| Crop | Ideal pH |
|---|---|
| Strawberry (alpine) | 5.5–6.2 |
| Paprika / Dulce Italiano / chili | 5.8–6.3 |
| Basil / parsley / dill | 5.5–6.5 |
| Lettuce | 5.5–6.0 |

## Commissioning sequence

State 2026-07-27: reservoir holds **7.5 L plain demineralised water** — EC ~0,
unbuffered pH. Demin water is unbuffered, so pH readings before nutrients go
in are meaningless (they drift).

1. Add A+B nutrients (on hand). Target **EC 0.8–1.0** for the first young
   transplants, not the full 1.4–1.6 — that comes later as plants mature.
2. Measure pH with the PH-201H pen (nutrients buffer the water, readings now
   stable). Later the SEN0169-V2 probe provides continuous readings.
3. Correct toward pH 6.0 with pH-Down/Up (to buy).
4. Germinate seeds in starter sponges; transplant into pods once roots show
   through the sponge. See [seeding/](seeding/README.md).
5. Strawberries are **seed-grown alpines** ('Rote Baron Solemacher', on
   hand) — sown first because they are the slowest crop, and they make the
   grow light mandatory by mid-September. See
   [seeding/strawberry.md](seeding/strawberry.md).

## Ops notes

- 7 fruiting plants on 10 L → expect near-daily top-ups once mature in
  summer; the water-level sensor (#220) makes this visible. Dropping the two
  truss tomatoes takes a real bite out of peak reservoir draw — they would
  have been the thirstiest plants in the tower by a wide margin.
- Top up with plain (demin) water between full nutrient changes; re-dose by
  EC reading.
- Lettuce at the shared EC 1.4–1.6 still risks some **tip burn** — harvest
  heads young and keep airflow over the tower. Less of a problem than at
  1.6–1.8, but the one-reservoir compromise has not vanished.
