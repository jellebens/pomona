# DFR0523 dosing pumps — as-built (cards #284–#287)

Four DFRobot Gravity Digital Peristaltic Pumps (DFR0523, integrated driver,
BPT acid-resistant tube) dose the reservoir. The GIGA decides and times every
dose; the pumps only ever run from explicit commands (bench module, v1.3.x)
or — later — the #224 controller with its rails.

## Channel table (as-built)

Constants below are the 2026-09-09 recalibration on the FINAL wiring and
tube runs (perm plumbing changed the numbers a lot — see the note under the
table). Bench-era values in parentheses for history only; never dose with
them.

| ch | reagent | GIGA pin | full speed (100) | slow (speed 50) | calibrated | status |
|----|---------|----------|------------------|-----------------|------------|--------|
| 1 | pH-Down | **D6** | **0.48 ml/s** (4.8 ml/10 s + 5.2 ml over 4×2.74 s; bench 0.73) | **0.11 ml/s** (3.4 ml over 3×10 s; bench 0.26) | 2026-09-09 | ✅ commissioned |
| 2 | Nutrient A | **D4** | **0.66 ml/s** (9.9 ml over 3×5 s + 3.2 ml/5 s; bench 0.50) | **0.24 ml/s** (7.2 ml over 3×10 s; = bench) | 2026-09-09 | ✅ commissioned |
| 3 | Nutrient B | **D5** | **0.60 ml/s** (6.2 ml/10 s + 7.8 ml over 4×3.33 s; = bench) | **0.11 ml/s** (3.2 ml over 3×10 s; bench 0.20) | 2026-09-09 | ✅ commissioned |
| 4 | spare | D7 (reserved) | – | – | – | pending #287 — NO healthy spare pump left, see below |

**The perm plumbing changed the constants — recalibrate after ANY tube or
routing change.** Full-speed: ch1 fell 0.73→0.48 (−35%, its run costs the
most lift), ch2 rose 0.50→0.66, ch3 held. Slow speed: ch1 and ch3 both
halved to 0.11 (low-RPM flow is hit much harder by lift/backpressure) while
ch2 held 0.24. Per-channel, per-speed spread is real — never assume one
channel's constant for another, and never interpolate between speeds.

Short dosed shots deliver slightly less per second than a continuous run
(start/stop transients): ch3 measured 0.59 ml/s over 4×3.3 s doses vs 0.62
continuous. For sub-5 s doses expect ~3-5% under the table rate.

Ch1 also measured (bench era) at speed 75: barely above speed 50 — the
speed→flow curve is nearly flat through the middle then leaps to the top:
use speed 50 for fine doses and speed 100 for volume, nothing in between.

**Break-in drift (measured on ch3, 2026-09-05):** a fresh tube climbs while
it beds in (~10% over the first minutes). Run a new tube ~1 min at full
speed before calibrating, and RECALIBRATE every channel after the first
week of real use.

⚠ **PWM pin capability (measured 2026-09-09):** the 2026-09-09 perm-wiring
attempt moved the signals to D10–D13 and only ch2 (D10) ever spun. Metering
each terminal showed **D11, D12 and D13 stuck at ~3.3 V** — no servo frames
at all (a healthy pin averages ~0.09 V full-forward / ~0.24 V at stop /
~0.4 V full-reverse); those pins have no mbed `PwmOut` timer route on the
GIGA. **Dosing signals live on D4–D7 only** (all four bench-verified);
D10 also works but is kept free. Never land a PPM wire on D11–D13.

**Direction (ch1): green tube = intake, blue tube = outlet, when running
`fwd`.** Tape arrows on the head match.

Dose timing at the 2026-09-09 rates, per 1 ml:
ch1 pH-Down ≈ 2.1 s full / 9.1 s slow; ch2 A ≈ 1.5 s full / 4.2 s slow;
ch3 B ≈ 1.7 s full / 9.1 s slow. The speed→flow response is NONLINEAR —
never interpolate, calibrate each speed you use.

## Wiring (per pump)

Gravity 3-pin cable + LRS-50-5 (5 V/10 A, back panel, terminal cover ON):

- **red → LRS `+V`**, **black → LRS `−V`** (pump peak 2.5 A — NEVER from the GIGA)
- **green/signal → GIGA screw-shield terminal D4–D7** (per channel table)
- **one shared wire LRS `−V` → GIGA GND** — the signal reference. Without it
  the PPM line means nothing and the pump ignores everything (bench-proven).
- Yellow-wire note: the DFR0523 cable is 3-pin (no mode wire — that's the
  A02YYUW level sensor, don't confuse the pigtails).

⚠ **Polarity kills.** Pump #1's driver board was destroyed by a red/black
reversal on 2026-09-03 (its motor survived — kept as parts donor:
motor + head spin fine on direct 5 V at `M+`/`M−`; a €2 MOSFET module can
resurrect it as an on/off-only pump, e.g. a demin top-up dribbler). One
replacement DFR0523 goes on the next TinyTronics order to restore the spare.

## Control signal (the hard-won part)

Standard 50 Hz servo PPM, pulse values copied bit-for-bit from DFRobot's own
GravityPump library (`Servo.attach(pin)` defaults + `write(0..180)`):

| action | pulse |
|--------|-------|
| max forward | **544 µs** |
| stop | **1472 µs** |
| max reverse | **2400 µs** |

**Never use the Arduino Servo library on the GIGA** — it holds the pin
near-constantly HIGH (measured ~3 V average on D4 where a servo frame
averages 0.1–0.4 V). The firmware generates frames with mbed `PwmOut`
(`period_ms(20)` + `pulsewidth_us(...)`) instead. 3.3 V logic is sufficient —
no level shifter needed (bench-verified).

## Commands (bench module, firmware ≥1.3.2)

- MQTT: publish `pomona/dose/test` → `chN fwd|rev|stop [ms] [speed 1-100]`;
  results (and completions) on retained `pomona/dose/result`.
- Serial (115200): `dose chN fwd|rev|stop [ms] [speed]`, `dose status`
  (per-channel pin/pulse/run state), `help`. Dosing events tag as
  `[PUMP001]`…`[PUMP004]`, MQTT lifecycle as `[MQTT]` (≥ the release after
  1.3.2 for the tags).
- Hard cap 10 s per command (`DOSE_TEST_MAX_MS`), one channel at a time,
  every channel held at explicit stop (1472 µs) from boot.

## Calibration procedure (repeat per channel, and after any tube change)

1. Prime: `ch N fwd 10000` at full speed with intake in water (an air-gapped
   line under-measures — always prime before measuring).
2. Three 10 s runs into a measuring cup at speed 100 → mean = full ml/s
   (ch1 spread was ±1.4%).
3. One or more 10 s runs at each slow speed you intend to use.
4. Record in the channel table with the date, like `EC_CAL_K` and the pH
   anchors.

Plumbing rules (from #224's design): pumps mounted above the waterline,
lines loop up-then-down (anti-siphon), check valve at the tank end, rigid
intake tube/weight in the reagent bottle, A and B never share any fitting.
